## Backend/mobile and editorial contract

Implement a Phase 1 traveler projection that omits monetary fields and money-bearing formatted text before render. Choose a backward-compatible endpoint/DTO projection or capability policy without breaking unrelated clients or altering admin UI. Keep the existing stored money columns and internal provenance. If monetary fields must remain in an existing general API for compatibility, use an explicit Phase 1 server response projection and mobile view-model allowlist rather than passing those general records directly to UI. Do not treat hiding a widget as the complete contract.

Add or reuse a suitable explicit qualitative preference field with stable values equivalent to `low | medium | luxury`. Display localized labels (suggested VI: Thấp / Trung bình / Cao cấp; EN: Low / Medium / Luxury), with no amount or range subtitles. Define mapping from legacy `cheap/balanced/premium` only for existing qualitative records. Do not infer preferences from numeric budgets or overwrite old amounts. A legacy trip with no qualitative preference may remain unset until the user chooses; opening/editing its name must not clear stored money fields or trigger new currency validation.

For new creation, omit monetary request fields; any legacy required field must be handled compatibly on the backend, not exposed as hidden fabricated values. Keep valid nonfinancial numbers: dates, durations, distance, group size, ratings and counts. Do not strip every digit from content. Reject or hold monetary material from traveler publication using structured field exclusion plus editorial review/content validation. Currency-symbol regex alone misses words, ranges and formatted prose and is not a complete solution.

Source research can retain prices privately for provenance and future use. The three staged files remain **internal editorial research**, not traveler-ready payloads. Produce reviewed Phase 1 derivatives with all amounts and pricing sections removed before publishing, while preserving citations/operational context for non-price claims. Do not erase Library originals or research notes. Remove price-oriented advice/labels from traveler copy instead of replacing it with “price unavailable” or “free.” Safety, access and schedule guidance can remain if independently useful and properly qualified.
